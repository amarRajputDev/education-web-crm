AAROHAN INSTITUTE OF HIGHER EDUCATION
Admission Checklist (Demo Document)
===================================

Keep the following ready before you submit an online application.

DOCUMENTS
---------
[ ] Recent passport-size photograph (JPEG/PNG, max 2 MB)
[ ] Identity proof — Aadhaar, passport or school ID (PDF/JPG, max 5 MB)
[ ] Last qualifying mark sheet (PDF/JPG, max 5 MB)
[ ] Transfer certificate / migration certificate
[ ] Category certificate, if claiming reservation or concession
[ ] Character certificate from the last institution

ELIGIBILITY QUICK VIEW
----------------------
* Undergraduate programmes: 10+2 with a minimum of 50% aggregate.
* Postgraduate programmes: Bachelor's degree with 50–55% aggregate.
* Diploma in MLT: 10+2 with Biology or Chemistry, minimum 45%.
* B.Des: 10+2 plus portfolio review / studio aptitude exercise.

STEPS
-----
1. Choose your programme on the Courses page.
2. Fill the six-step application (personal, academic, programme, address,
   documents, review).
3. Note the generated application reference number.
4. Track the status on the Track Application page.
