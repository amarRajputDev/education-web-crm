SCHOLARSHIP CATEGORIES
----------------------
* Merit scholarship — up to 25% of tuition for 85%+ in 10+2.
* Early-bird concession — 10% for applications before the first counselling window.
* Need-based aid — up to 30% of tuition for verified family income criteria.
* Sibling and defence-ward concessions as per institute policy.

PAYMENT
-------
Tuition fees are payable semester-wise. Education loan assistance and staged
payment plans are available on request from the accounts office.
